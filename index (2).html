Regional Data to Policy (D2P)
Training of Trainers Workshop

September 28 - October 1, 2026 | Phuket, Thailand

Building sustainable capacity for evidence-informed policy development in Bangladesh and Thailand

Welcome to the Regional D2P TOT Workshop

The Data to Policy (D2P) program, developed by Vital Strategies, the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the CDC Foundation as part of the Bloomberg Philanthropies Data for Health Initiative, aims to bridge the data-policy gap through team-based training and mentoring of health policy professionals in low- to middle-income countries.

Workshop Objectives

By the end of the TOT workshop, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the components of D2P
  • Define their roles as facilitators and mentors
  • Apply instructional skills to facilitate D2P courses
  • Respond to common facilitation challenges
  • Conduct end-of-day participant debriefs
  • Mentor D2P participants through all phases of the D2P course
  • Handle mentoring challenges related to developing D2P skills

Program Background

D2P participants develop data-driven policy briefs and recommendations that respond to government health priorities. The program employs several types of analysis—including economic evaluation of policy options—to inform policy recommendations.

To ensure the program's long-term sustainability, the CDC Foundation is hosting this Training of Trainers (TOT) workshop specifically for Bangladesh and Thailand. This regional TOT model enhances country-specific TOTs, optimizing resources while cultivating a shared pool of skilled in-country and regional mentors and facilitators capable of independently leading future D2P cohorts, and fostering collaboration among Data Impact focus countries.

Participating Countries

This workshop brings together participants from CDC Foundation Data Impact Program focus countries:

  • Bangladesh
  • Thailand

Approximately 10 participants, including four nominated D2P mentors each from Bangladesh and Thailand.

Key Topics

  • Overview of D2P and module summaries
  • Facilitation and mentoring skills, with practice on root-cause analysis, problem statements, policy options, and economic modules
  • Handling mentoring challenges through activities like "Pass the Buck" and policy brief reviews
  • Wrap-up sessions with debriefs and evaluations to ensure learning consolidation

Eligibility & Nomination

Ideal candidates are graduates of the D2P course, selected by their local government or nominated by DI country coordinators based on performance and demonstrated interest in facilitation and mentoring. Participants should be mid-level public health professionals from Ministries of Health, public health institutes, or national universities, with expertise in data analysis, program planning, or health policy development.

Eligibility criteria include:

  • Prior completion of a D2P course or equivalent experience in data-driven policy work
  • Commitment to facilitating or mentoring future D2P courses in their country
  • Endorsement from the Ministry of Health or a relevant government body
  • No prior participation in any D2P TOT workshop
  • No previous experience as a mentor or facilitator in D2P cohorts
  • Availability for the full workshop duration and follow-up activities

Bangladesh and Thailand are encouraged to nominate up to four candidates each, prioritizing those who can contribute to building a sustainable cadre of trainers.
